# C — RELAT Brand Brief

## 1. Brand

**Name:** RELAT  
**Domain:** relat.studio  
**Descriptor:** Digital Studio  
**Location:** Santa Teresa, Costa Rica

RELAT is a creative digital studio combining strategy, design, development and creative technology.

---

## 2. Positioning

**RELAT is a digital studio for people and businesses that care about how their brand exists online.**

RELAT combines:

**strategy × design × technology × creativity**

RELAT is not primarily:

- a traditional web-development company
- a marketing agency
- a branding agency
- a template-based web design studio
- a large corporate digital agency

It is a small, highly involved creative technology studio.

---

## 3. Brand Philosophy

### RELATE

Understand the person, business and audience.

### MAKE

Turn ideas into something real.

### FEEL

Digital experiences should create a feeling.

### MOVE

Interaction should make the experience feel alive.

### CARE

Small details matter.

---

## 4. Audience

Primary:

- independent businesses
- creative entrepreneurs
- hospitality
- restaurants
- surf businesses
- wellness
- artists
- musicians
- photographers
- designers
- lifestyle brands
- tourism businesses
- premium local businesses

These are markets, not restrictions.

RELAT should not position itself exclusively as a hospitality or artist agency.

---

## 5. Personality

RELAT should feel:

- Creative
- Confident
- Human
- Sophisticated
- Experimental
- Technical
- Warm
- Minimal
- Thoughtful
- Cinematic

---

## 6. Voice

Use:

- short sentences
- clear language
- confident simplicity
- thoughtful statements
- occasional conceptual language

Avoid:

- corporate jargon
- agency clichés
- exaggerated claims
- "cutting-edge solutions"
- "digital transformation"
- "unlock your potential"
- excessive technical terminology

RELAT should sound like a creative person who understands technology.

---

## 7. Visual Direction

### Editorial × Cinematic × Digital × Human

Balance:

**large typography + negative space + movement + tactile imagery + subtle technology**

---

## 8. Typography

Typography should be one of the main visual tools.

Use:

- large expressive headlines
- oversized type
- strong hierarchy
- generous spacing
- restrained body copy
- typography as graphic composition

---

## 9. Motion

Use:

- scroll-based transitions
- image reveals
- scale changes
- hover isolation
- image expansion
- background transitions
- subtle parallax
- floating/elevation effects
- cursor interactions
- section continuity

Motion must feel intentional and cinematic.

---

## 10. Hero

The hero should create:

**personality + confidence + curiosity**

It should not explain everything.

Use:

- oversized typography
- strong composition
- distinctive visual element
- subtle motion
- generous negative space

The hero should make visitors want to scroll.

---

## 11. Color

Keep the color system flexible.

Use strong contrast and controlled transitions.

Possible palette behavior:

- neutral
- dark
- light
- warm
- occasional saturated accents

Color changes can become part of the interaction.

---

## 12. Sun / Light Motif

Use the original Sunlight concept only as a subtle visual influence.

Possible applications:

- warm highlights
- glow
- lens flare
- sunlight through imagery
- circular forms
- luminosity transitions

Do not create a literal sun-centered logo.

---

## 13. Photography

Photography should feel:

- cinematic
- authentic
- atmospheric
- human
- slightly imperfect
- emotionally evocative

Avoid generic stock imagery.

---

## 14. Brand Anti-References

Avoid:

- generic SaaS
- excessive futuristic interfaces
- AI-generated-looking branding
- architecture aesthetics
- fashion aesthetics
- excessive glassmorphism
- excessive 3D
- excessive text
- corporate technology branding
- generic creative-agency visuals

---

## 15. Core Brand Idea

> **Good digital work starts with understanding.**

The brand should communicate that RELAT understands the person, business, audience, feeling and technology before building.
