# B — RELAT Brand Strategy

## 1. Brand Foundation

**Name:** RELAT  
**Domain:** relat.studio  
**Descriptor:** Digital Studio

RELAT comes from the idea of **relate**: to understand, connect, communicate and create through a relationship.

The name is intentionally abstract enough to grow beyond a single service.

---

## 2. Brand Idea

### Good digital work starts with understanding.

Before designing or developing, RELAT seeks to understand:

- the person
- the business
- the audience
- the message
- the desired feeling
- the practical objective

The result should feel considered rather than assembled.

---

## 3. Brand Promise

RELAT turns ideas, identities and businesses into thoughtful digital experiences.

The promise is not that every project will be experimental.

The promise is that every project will be:

- intentional
- well designed
- technically sound
- collaborative
- distinctive
- appropriate to the client

---

## 4. Brand Territory

RELAT lives at the intersection of:

**Human × Digital**

**Strategy × Creativity**

**Design × Technology**

**Stillness × Movement**

**Function × Feeling**

This tension should be visible throughout the identity and website.

---

## 5. Brand Personality

### Creative

RELAT has a point of view.

### Human

The studio should feel approachable and collaborative.

### Confident

It should communicate expertise without over-explaining.

### Sophisticated

The visual language should feel considered rather than decorative.

### Experimental

RELAT can explore unusual interactions and visual ideas.

### Technical

The execution should feel reliable and precise.

### Warm

The brand should avoid cold technology aesthetics.

### Minimal

Every element should have a reason.

---

## 6. Personality Spectrum

RELAT should generally sit here:

**Human ← RELAT → Technical**

rather than:

**Corporate ← RELAT → Experimental**

The brand can be experimental visually while remaining grounded and useful.

---

## 7. Brand Archetype

RELAT is primarily a combination of:

### The Creator

Makes things, explores ideas and values originality.

### The Explorer

Looks for new ways of presenting information and creating digital experiences.

### The Sage

Understands that good work starts with observation and thought.

The brand should not become overly intellectual or serious. The Creator remains dominant.

---

## 8. Emotional Goals

The brand should make people feel:

- curious
- inspired
- comfortable
- confident
- interested
- slightly surprised

The desired reaction is:

> "This feels different."

followed by:

> "These people know what they are doing."

---

## 9. Verbal Identity

RELAT communicates with clarity and restraint.

### Prefer

- direct statements
- short sentences
- strong verbs
- specific language
- conceptual statements used sparingly

### Avoid

- buzzwords
- corporate jargon
- excessive adjectives
- generic agency claims
- technical flexing
- fake urgency
- exaggerated promises

---

## 10. Naming Logic

RELAT intentionally modifies the word **relate**.

This gives the brand:

- a familiar conceptual root
- a distinctive visual form
- a more ownable name
- flexibility across industries

The missing final "e" should not be explained constantly.

The meaning can emerge through the brand's language and behavior.

---

## 11. Descriptor

Preferred:

**RELAT — Digital Studio**

The descriptor provides context while allowing RELAT to remain the primary brand.

Avoid making "Studio" part of the core name when possible.

The domain should remain:

**relat.studio**

---

## 12. Visual Concept

### Editorial × Cinematic × Digital × Human

The identity should combine:

- large typography
- negative space
- strong imagery
- controlled contrast
- movement
- subtle digital behavior
- warm atmospheric details

It should feel like a creative studio, not a software company.

---

## 13. Motion as Brand Language

Motion is not an optional decoration.

It is one of the ways RELAT communicates.

Movement can represent:

- connection
- progression
- rhythm
- discovery
- transformation

The website should therefore feel alive without becoming visually noisy.

---

## 14. Light as a Secondary Motif

The previous Sunlight identity can survive as an atmospheric influence.

Do not preserve "sunlight" as a literal brand concept.

Instead use:

- warm light
- glow
- luminosity
- reflections
- gradients
- circular light forms
- light/dark transitions

This creates continuity with the original concept while allowing RELAT to become its own brand.

---

## 15. Reference Map

### Locomotive

Typography, artistic hover interactions and unconventional composition.

### Instrument

Background transitions, hover isolation, scaling and elevation.

### Studio Freight

Interactive project presentation and image exploration.

### Nagi

Cinematic hero and scroll-based animation.

Use interaction principles, not hospitality styling.

### VM Concepts

Large typography, rhythm and balance between motion and whitespace.

### J T Hospitality

Hero atmosphere, navbar placement and strong animation.

Use interaction principles, not hospitality styling.

### Cure

Loader and opening experience.

Do not copy the inconsistent visual language later in the site.

---

## 16. What the Brand Should Avoid

RELAT should not resemble:

- generic SaaS
- generic AI startup
- architecture studio
- fashion agency
- traditional marketing agency
- corporate consulting firm
- template marketplace
- overly futuristic technology brand

Avoid excessive:

- gradients
- glassmorphism
- 3D objects
- rounded cards
- neon
- stock imagery
- copy-heavy layouts

---

## 17. Brand North Star

When a design decision is uncertain, ask:

> Does this make RELAT feel more human, more thoughtful, more expressive and more intentional?

If yes, continue.

If it only makes the website look more complicated, reconsider it.
