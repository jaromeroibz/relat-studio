# A — RELAT Business & Audience Strategy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic foundation for RELAT: who the studio is for, how it should position itself, where it should begin, and how it can grow without becoming overly niche.

It should guide both RELAT's own website and future client strategy.

---

## 2. Business Context

RELAT is a small creative digital studio based in Santa Teresa, Costa Rica.

The founder has a strong background in music and creative work and enjoys collaborating with artists and creative people. This creative perspective is an advantage, but RELAT should not limit itself to being an "agency for artists."

The business should initially leverage the local network and market in Santa Teresa while building a brand that can expand across Costa Rica and internationally.

---

## 3. The Central Strategic Decision

RELAT should avoid choosing between:

- "artist-focused agency"
- "generic web development agency"

Instead, position itself around a **way of working and a type of client**, rather than a single industry.

The ideal client is someone who values:

- design
- originality
- quality
- collaboration
- personality
- a strong digital presence

This allows RELAT to work with artists, hospitality businesses, restaurants, lifestyle brands, professionals and other businesses without making the brand feel unfocused.

---

## 4. Initial Audience

### Primary audience

Independent businesses, creative entrepreneurs and small-to-medium businesses that care about how their brand exists online.

Examples:

- boutique hospitality
- restaurants
- surf businesses
- wellness
- artists
- musicians
- photographers
- designers
- creative professionals
- lifestyle brands
- tourism businesses
- premium local businesses

These are initial opportunities, not permanent categories.

### Secondary audience

Businesses in Costa Rica outside Santa Teresa that need a stronger digital presence and are willing to invest in quality.

Potential markets:

- San José
- Tamarindo
- Nosara
- Guanacaste
- other tourism and creative markets

### Long-term audience

International clients looking for a small, highly involved creative digital studio.

---

## 5. Why Not Position as an Artist Agency?

The founder's creative and musical background gives RELAT credibility with artists and creative people.

However, making artists the primary niche would create several limitations:

- smaller addressable market
- greater price sensitivity
- risk of becoming a personal portfolio rather than a studio
- difficulty expanding into established businesses
- weaker positioning for corporate or premium clients

Artists can remain an important audience without becoming the brand's defining category.

---

## 6. Why Not Become a Generic Tech Agency?

A conventional development positioning would make RELAT compete primarily on:

- technical capability
- features
- speed
- price
- number of technologies
- service lists

That would erase one of RELAT's biggest advantages: creative judgment.

RELAT should use technology as a tool rather than as the identity.

The client should think:

> "They understand design and technology."

Not:

> "They are developers who can also make a website look good."

---

## 7. Positioning

### Core positioning

**RELAT is a creative digital studio for people and businesses that care about how their brand exists online.**

RELAT combines:

**strategy + design + development + creative technology**

to create digital experiences that are visually distinctive, intuitive and purposeful.

---

## 8. Differentiation

RELAT differentiates through:

### Creative thinking

Projects begin with understanding rather than immediately selecting a template.

### Personal collaboration

A small studio allows direct communication and involvement.

### Design quality

The website itself should demonstrate a high level of visual craft.

### Technical execution

Creative ideas must become reliable, responsive, maintainable websites.

### Motion and interaction

RELAT treats movement and interaction as part of communication, not decoration.

### Flexibility

The studio can work across industries without being locked into one niche.

---

## 9. Ideal Client Profile

The ideal RELAT client:

- cares about their brand
- wants something better than a template
- values design
- is willing to collaborate
- understands that a website is part of the business
- has a clear product, service or creative identity
- wants a long-term digital partner rather than only a developer

RELAT should avoid competing primarily on price.

---

## 10. Client Qualification

Strong potential clients usually have at least three of these characteristics:

- clear business identity
- existing visual identity or strong sense of direction
- quality product/service
- willingness to invest
- desire for differentiation
- need for better digital presentation
- appreciation for creative work

Weak-fit clients tend to:

- compare only on price
- want a cheap template
- provide no content or direction
- expect unlimited revisions
- treat the website as a commodity

---

## 11. Geographic Strategy

### Phase 1 — Santa Teresa

Build reputation through:

- local relationships
- referrals
- portfolio projects
- visible local businesses
- creative network

The advantage of Santa Teresa is density of independent businesses, international visitors, hospitality, wellness, surf and creative culture.

### Phase 2 — Costa Rica

Use the initial portfolio and referrals to reach other markets.

### Phase 3 — International

The website, portfolio and brand should already feel international enough that location does not become a limitation.

---

## 12. Growth Strategy

RELAT should grow through reputation rather than trying to look like a large agency.

Priority order:

1. Build excellent projects.
2. Document them well.
3. Develop a recognizable visual language.
4. Generate referrals.
5. Build relationships with complementary professionals.
6. Increase project value.
7. Expand geographically.

Potential future partnerships:

- photographers
- videographers
- brand designers
- copywriters
- SEO specialists
- developers
- marketing professionals

---

## 13. Strategic Principle

RELAT should be **specialized in taste, not restricted by industry**.

The common denominator is not:

> "We work with artists."

It is:

> "We work with people and businesses that care about what they put into the world."

---

## 14. Success Criteria

The strategy is working if:

- clients approach RELAT because of its visual quality
- the studio is not primarily competing on price
- portfolio projects span multiple industries
- the brand remains coherent across those industries
- clients describe RELAT as creative, thoughtful and technically strong
- the business can expand beyond Santa Teresa without rebranding
